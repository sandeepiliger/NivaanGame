Privacy Policy

BrainySparks · Last updated August 2026

The short version

This is a learning game for young children, and we have tried to keep it as private as an app can reasonably be. Your child's name, avatar, stars and progress never leave the device — there is no account, no login, and no server of ours that they are sent to. The app does show ads and does measure anonymous usage, and those two things are described in full below.

Who we are

BrainySparks is an educational game for children aged roughly two to eight, covering numbers, letters, colours, shapes and logic puzzles. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

What stays on the device

Everything to do with your child's play is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet. It is never uploaded to us, and we cannot see it. This includes:

  • The profile name and avatar you choose (you may use a nickname, and we suggest you do)
  • Stars earned, levels completed, and which skills have been practised
  • Rewards unlocked and daily streak counts
  • Your settings, including sound, music, voice and break reminders

Uninstalling the app erases all of it permanently. You can also clear it at any time from the grown-ups area, using “Reset progress”.

Anonymous usage measurement

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used in aggregate — for example, which worlds children play most and where levels are too hard. This helps us decide what to improve.

The events we record are things like a screen being opened, a level being started or finished, and a reward being unlocked. Firebase also collects standard technical information such as device model, operating system version, and approximate country. We do not send your child's name, their avatar, or any other information you typed into the app. We cannot use any of this to identify a particular child.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. Because this is an app for children, we have configured AdMob so that:

  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent
  • Only non-personalised ads are requested, so ads are not targeted using your child's activity or an advertising identifier
  • Ad content is restricted to the “General” audience rating, the most restrictive setting available
  • No full-screen ads are ever shown in the toddler world, where a very young child could not reliably dismiss one

Google still processes limited technical data in order to serve and measure ads, in line with its own policies for child-directed apps. You can read how Google handles this at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Removing ads

The grown-ups area offers a one-time purchase that removes all ads. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play — we never see or store your card details, billing address, or Google account. The app only records, on your own device, that the purchase was made.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for children, and we have designed it to comply with the Google Play Families policy and with children's privacy laws including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no sign-up, no profile that we hold, no messaging, no photo or microphone access, and no way for a child to communicate with anyone through the app. Settings and purchases are placed behind a simple parental gate.

What we never do

  • We never ask for an email address, phone number, or real name
  • We never collect location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • We never sell or rent data to anyone
  • We never show personalised or behaviourally targeted advertising
  • We never include social media links, chat, or user-to-user features

Your choices

  • Remove ads entirely with the one-time purchase in the grown-ups area.
  • Erase all progress using “Reset progress”, also in the grown-ups area.
  • Remove everything by uninstalling the app, which deletes all local data.
  • Limit ad tracking through your device's own Android privacy settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and change the date shown at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.

Contact us

If you have any question about this policy or about your child's privacy, please write to us at iligerstudios@gmail.com and we will respond as quickly as we can.
